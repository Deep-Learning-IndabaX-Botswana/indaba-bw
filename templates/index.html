Mission & Key Programmes

Inspired by the Indaba's mission, we saw the potential to ignite Botswana's AI community. A team of passionate AI professionals and enthusiasts came together, determined to make this event community-driven and impactful.

  • Spread scientific knowledge amongst researchers
  • Showcase cutting-edge applications from industry partners
  • Provide a practical introduction to deep learning for beginners
  • Build a community of responsible AI practitioners
The IndabaX is free to attend for students!
